Improvements:
- Editor with timeline and other tools

Known Bugs:
- If you quickly switch between multiple projects it will crash the app (to try to prevent this the list of projects is disabled very briefly after selecting a project)
- There are some inputs into the editor that will cause crashes or different perspectives to just appear black

Current features:
- Mac (14.0+), iPad (17.0+) landscape-only
- 1.0 and 2.0 of the original scene (binaries thinned down significantly to allow for upload)
- Ability to create a project and name it
- Ability to switch between projects and all views in the app reflect that state change
- Ability to view individual perspectives in the Playback screen
- Ability to create an edited project through the combination of multiple perspectives using a timeline and other tools
- Audio and video is perfectly synced through play/pause and seeking in playback and editor views
- Persistence of projects and edits throughout app launches

Issues:
- App size is still quite large
- No ability to edit project information in the list and the viewable information is very simple
- The app is locked to landscape and iPad/Mac only
